# Phase 2 — Document ingestion beyond prospectuses

Prepared on the Phase 1 base `00f1fb1`. This plan is review material: implementation needs the maintainer's review of this concrete plan and a formal start through the handoff skill (checkpoint, `in_progress`, ledger pointers). `preparation_only: true` keeps the ledger at active phase none. Nothing here is institutional approval; audit success, EXTRACTED, VERIFIED and content review are never approval of a source or a curriculum. Items not run are marked NOT RUN.

## Goal and authoritative context

Master section 7 (7.1 steps 1-9, 7.2 chunk policy, 7.3 ten-scanned-document check): produce source-linked candidate text, chunks and an ingestion report from the institutional originals, with no embedding, vector write, executable Prolog or runtime routing change.

- Input: the 78 originals under `E:\Bintanong\institutional-sources\originals`, enumerated read-only on 2026-10-09 (names, counts, extensions only): 78 files, 59 PDF + 17 JPG + 2 PPTX, longest full path 220 characters. Folders: Citizens Charter 8 (6 PDF, 2 PPTX), Memos and other documents 6 JPG, Procedures 16 (5 PDF, 11 JPG), Student Handbook and Constitutions 4 PDF, prospectus dump 44 PDF. Byte identities, duplicate groups (63 unique, 13 groups) and acquisition hashes come from the external inventory and are NOT re-verified by this plan.
- The 44 prospectus PDFs belong to the parallel prospectus workstream. Task 1 counts and health-checks them; no policy ingestion re-runs them.
- Output kind: candidates only. Every source stays `verification pending`, approval null; issuer, campus/college scope, effectivity, supersession and conflicts need authorization evidence the maintainer has not supplied. CSG evidence is missing and is never inferred from USG.
- Consumed from Phase 1: `backend/bintanong_contracts` (SourceSpan, Chunk, TokenCount, RegisterBinding, `bind_chunk`, `check_chunk_binding`, SourceDocumentVersion, SourceConflict), `knowledge/manifests/governance-vocabulary.json`, the pending-source traceability pattern in `docs/decisions/phase-01-traceability.md`, and the evaluation protocol in `evaluation/README.md`.
