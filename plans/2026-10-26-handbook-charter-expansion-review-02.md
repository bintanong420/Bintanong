### A15 — face-to-face admission, complete sequence

1. Secure an application form for admission from OSAS.
2. Fill out correctly/completely and submit with the source-specific requirements:
   freshman — photocopy of High School Card, 2 pieces 1x1 ID picture, Official
   Receipt for admission fee payment (University Cashier’s Office); transferee —
   photocopy of Transcript of Records or Honorable Dismissal/Certification of
   Grades, 2 pieces 1x1 ID picture, Official Receipt for admission fee payment
   (University Cashier’s Office); foreign applicant — Scholastic Record/Transcript
   of Records duly authenticated, 2 pieces 1x1 ID picture, Official Receipt of
   payment for Admission Fee (University Cashier’s Office), Birth Certificate
   (Authenticated Copy), ACR, Student Visa, and English proficiency test if native
   language or medium of instruction is not English. Last two continue on H111.
3. OSAS reviews completeness/validity, schedules examination and releases permit.
4. Applicant reports at arranged date/time/place with College Admission Test
   Permit, School ID and pencil No. 2; GC/OP administers the test.
5. Return to OSAS for result, “on or before the first week of May (for the First
   Batch)”. Preserve the historical first-batch qualifier; no current-year date.
6. Foreign applicant only: admission officer prepares/issues NOA and advises on
   enrolment requirements. NOA does not itself prove visa/enrolment approval.

H110 diagram: requirements/admission-form submission at OSAS; “All requirements
attached?”; No -> “Attach required documents” -> submission; Yes -> scheduling ->
report on arranged date/time/place -> admission exam -> test results at OSAS -> END.
The diagram is a companion to all six textual steps, not a replacement that drops
foreign-applicant Step 6 or its H111 requirements continuation.
