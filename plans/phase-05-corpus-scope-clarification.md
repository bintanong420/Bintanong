# Phase 05 corpus and prospectus review clarification

Recorded 12 October 2026 after the owner asked why prospectus review was being
performed during retrieval and reported that the Citizens' Charter and student
handbook still needed to be added.

## Evidence and intended separation

`Context/bintanong_thesis_for_context.pdf`, physical pages 36, 55 and 68,
identifies the student handbook, Citizens' Charter and USG/CSG policies as the
core institutional retrieval corpus. Physical page 55 also describes prospectuses
among supplemental user-provided inputs, isolated from institutional knowledge.
The supplied runtime diagram separates RAG, Symbolic and Hybrid execution.

Master-plan sections 3.1, 6.1 and 11 additionally allow separately verified
institutional curriculum/prospectus material to support curriculum capabilities.
A student upload does not become an institutional source or install a rule.

Prospectus processing preserves original bytes, page/cell provenance, extracted
course fields and curriculum scope. Reviewed structured facts can later feed
Phase 06 rule specifications and reproducible Prolog compilation. Source-linked
prospectus text can also support retrieval and Hybrid explanations under the
existing plan. Retrieval of a prerequisite statement is distinct from computing
a student's eligibility using that statement.

Handbook, Charter, memoranda and other authorized policy documents provide
retrievable procedures, explanatory clauses, conditions and exceptions. Explicit
formalizable rules from these sources can also feed Phase 06; document category
alone does not determine the runtime route.

## What the current review establishes

The 27-case prospectus packet is a development source-reading and citation
exercise. It can check extracted answers and scope and prepare trustworthy
curriculum data for later symbolic work. It does not review Prolog logic,
execute eligibility rules or evaluate the missing handbook/Charter corpus.
Existing real-model measurements concern prospectus candidates only.

The recent workflow overemphasized the available prospectus corpus. Its
extraction defects remain legitimate curriculum blockers, but repairing every
one of the 39 prospectuses is not stated as the Phase 05 retrieval exit gate.
Master section 10 requires approved development evidence, unsupported-case
detection and resolvable citations for the evaluated scope. Missing or defective
sources must keep their corresponding capabilities unavailable. Their absence
does not invalidate unrelated retriever infrastructure.
