## Result and scope

The new administrative SelectedReleaseReviewLoader records an immutable exact
selection and binds it to an unpublished draft. Receipt sources, current C1
content/metadata/spans, complete ordered field coverage and same-source required
neighbors are checked locally and independently in SQL. Exact retries preserve
receipt identity; changed receipts, content or membership fail.

Explicitly bound publication can use matching partially_reviewed sources/chunks
without claiming whole-document completion. Independent verification/approval,
source/embedding provenance and active membership requirements remain. Whole-
reviewed releases retain their existing path. Runtime policies follow the same
acyclic membership/source/chunk order; administrative receipts are inaccessible.
No student request/result or calibration status change.

Undergraduate academic affairs remains the supported context. General student
procedures may apply without literal undergraduate wording; intended audience
and existing applicability evidence decide. Explicit Law, Medicine, graduate,
laboratory-school and staff-only procedures remain excluded. Staff actions
within a student service remain legitimate. Unknown applicability is not approved.
