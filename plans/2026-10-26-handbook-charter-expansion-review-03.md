## X018 — complete standing conditions and exceptions

H39 governing rule permits college rules subject to Academic Council approval and
the printed minima. Preserve the registered-units basis at last late-registration
day, distinct from units with final grades used by other clauses.

- Warning: failure to earn credit in 25%–49% registered academic units; Dean warning;
  failure to improve following semester -> probation.
- Probation: 50%–75% registered academic units; next semester. Removal by passing
  **more than** 50% units with final grades in succeeding semester; otherwise
  another semester; failure to remove for two consecutive semesters -> program
  disqualification. Carry H40 continuation: illness/similar-valid-reason INC not
  counted with verification documents to College Dean before next regular
  registration, excluding Summer.
- Dismissal: more than 75% but less than 100% registered academic units; dropped
  from program. Its own illness/valid-reason INC exception and same document/timing
  condition remain attached, not silently removed as duplicated wording.
- No advance courses with scholastic delinquency in regular semesters. Another
  University unit requires Counselor's aptitude/interest evaluation and permission,
  with probation; not automatic transfer after program dismissal.
- Permanent disqualification: failure in 100% of academic units with final grades;
  also repeat dismissal after admitted to another college. Preserve H41 exception:
  instructors' recommendation/faculty certification of unauthorized dropping rather
  than poor scholarship; after-midsemester dropping with poor class standing still
  counts grades 5. Dean/faculty committee considers individual merits, readmission
  on probation.
- Failed required subjects get next-enrollment priority. Dismissed-student
  readmission needs favorable OSAS Director recommendation and VPAA approval,
  decision final. Delinquency status entered in student record by institution.

No thresholds filled into the numerical gaps between printed percentages; do not
resolve overlapping year levels or determine standing from missing grades.
