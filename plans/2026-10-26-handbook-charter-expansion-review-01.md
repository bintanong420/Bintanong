```text
FOREIGN STUDENTS. Foreign students may be admitted to a degree program

enrolment shall be considered Filipinos until their graduation.
```

### A11 — Cross-enrollment and credit for outside study

```text

enrolled in another educational institution shall be allowed to cross enroll
in the University upon the approval of the Dean of the College in which
enrollment is sought and the University Registrar.
Cross-enrollment should be done during the registration period. A written
permit for cross-enrollment from the institution in which they are primarily

student’s other class schedule.
c) the subject is not a major nor a professional licensure
examination subject.
d) the subject has the same course description and number of units.
e) the number of units cross-enrolled does not exceed nine (9)
units.
No student is allowed to cross-enroll in two (2) or more schools outside
the University.
CREDIT FOR COURSE
CREDIT FOR COURSE TAKEN OUTSIDE THE UNIVERSITY. No credit shall be
```
