# Institutional source gaps and honest answers

Standing owner instruction,13October2026: university-provided content can be cut
off or defective. Record such occurrences and answer honestly; never hallucinate
the continuation of a broken process. This supplements the existing master plan
and undergraduate scope policy. It applies across sources and future runtime work.

Distinguish a transcription/extraction error from a defect in the original. When
the original visibly contains omitted text, preserve the raw reading/history and
derive the reviewed correction separately. When the original itself is incomplete,
unreadable, malformed or contradictory, retain that limitation. A human can confirm
the visible fragment without approving an invented completion or policy meaning.

Each affected item needs its source hash/version, physical page, field/region,
visible text, issue type, review/evidence references and affected claims. Record
known source blanks explicitly. Preserve gaps with any later chunk/retrieval/rule
derivative; a reference to the full PDF alone must not hide an incomplete process.
Related authorized evidence can resolve a gap only through a separately recorded
review decision. No automatic acquisition or assumptions from another program.

For a student question, use only supported visible content. When the missing or
ambiguous portion matters, say what the document does not provide and avoid claiming
a complete procedure, eligibility decision or sanction. Refer to the relevant
university office when its responsibility is supported by the available source;
do not invent contact details or office instructions. Unaffected verified facts
remain usable within their authorized audience and version.

Never interpret an absent field as zero, no charge, no prerequisite, no penalty,
no further steps, automatic approval or a completed workflow. Never silently repair
source grammar where it changes policy meaning. Unresolved conditions/exceptions
must not become executable rules. Evaluation must check the limitation and absence
of invented continuation as well as supported facts, under applicable TDD rules.

Graduation example: handbook physical64,row8 visibly says "diploma cover";
the review transcription omitted diploma and is corrected from original evidence.
Row6process label is partly clipped but the owner explicitly reads it as
"Update Student Records". Row9detail stops at "during" in the original; the
missing continuation and any process endpoint remain unknown. For a question
depending on the remaining steps, an acceptable response says the supplied
handbook cuts off there and refers to the University Registrar for confirmation.
It does not supply plausible graduation steps as institutional facts.

A recorded source defect is handled evidence, not a request for the owner to
reconstruct unavailable content. It blocks only claims relying on that gap;
other reviews and implementation can continue. Source authority/applicability,
runtime activation and phase gates remain separate. This records requirements
and review decisions; it does not claim runtime gap enforcement is implemented.
