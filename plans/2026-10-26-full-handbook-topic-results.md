## Exact examples and unresolved source issues

- p30,RE-ADMISSION:“Readmission of students who have not enrolled for at least one
  school year shall be subject to approval of the Dean of the College where they were
  last enrolled upon recommendation of the Director of Student Affairs.” Candidate,
  not an automatic readmission decision;retain its campus/requirements continuation.
- p56,STUDENT RECORDS:“A student may request correction of any item in a student
  record in accordance with University rules and regulations.” This supports a
  student process,not write access for Bintanong.
- pp113–114,STUDENT SCHOLARSHIP:Step5starts on113and its action continues on114.
  The printed note says acceptance does not automatically mean application approval.
  Keep the full procedure together;do not approve a partial first page as complete.
- pp120–121,GRIEVANCE/COMPLAINT:Step1mentions information about “the student or
  employee”;the next step assigns the practitioner to the applicant's college. This
  is a student complaint service,not a reason to exclude the whole procedure.
- p26,ADMISSION TO CLASSES:one definition requires fees plus COR/registration form;
  an asterisked definition mentions COR/form without that payment clause. The next
  line reads:“Except for the Nursing and Midwifery students are not exempted to pay
  the miscellaneous fees like RLE etc.” Preserve all;payment/exception interpretation
  remains unresolved. Do not turn it into an exemption,obligation calculation or
  definitive enrollment test. Exact complete lines were shown inline this session.
- p27:freshman requirements a–c are followed by “In addition to the above requirements,
  applicants seeking admission to the following programs must meet the enumerated
  requirements below:” then GRADUATE STUDENTS with no undergraduate list. The absence

  p29has a similar unfilled transferee/shifter program-list introduction. Preserve gaps.
- p30,APPLICANTS WITH ACADEMIC DEFICIENCES follows an explicitly graduate-probation
  paragraph before B.RE-ADMISSION. Its undergraduate applicability is unresolved;
  hold that paragraph separately rather than importing it with undergraduate readmission.
- p42,EXTENSION OF RESIDENCE follows the graduate-residence clauses and specifies
  master's/doctoral limits and extra graduate units. Do not infer an undergraduate
  extension entitlement from its generic opening.
- p47completion deadlines/grade consequences and p64graduation cutoff are already
  documented holds. Do not reopen settled readings,resolve precedence or invent the
