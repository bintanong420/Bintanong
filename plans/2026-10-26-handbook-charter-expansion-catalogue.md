   “Php 2.00/day”; C79 prints “Php 10.00/day for overdue.” H101 also ends that fine
   line with “Reserved Books” without a separate visible amount. Preserve all;
   neither applicability nor supersession is settled by the newer date alone.

   and total distinct; do not promise a completion deadline from their combination.
