# Bintanong benchmark questions (50)

Phase 12 benchmark, owner-approved 29 October 2026: 50 questions across four categories, 24 English, 16 Taglish
and 10 pure Tagalog (owner: pure-Tagalog questions are accepted; replies are English or Taglish). 30 reuse
owner-accepted gate questions that were also used during development (disclosed); the 10 disciplinary questions
come from printed Student Handbook penalty rows; the 10 Tagalog questions are owner-approved twins of Taglish ones.
"Must contain" lists the printed facts an answer needs (each group: any one alternative). "Expected" is the
required status or prerequisite decision. Result: full system (condition C) at `cb82c53` (Tagalog questions in their own run on the same code): **38/50**. See
[benchmark results](../../plans/2026-10-29-phase12-benchmark-results.md). Shorthand, broken English and the full
Tagalog set are separate robustness results: [accepted input forms](accepted-input-forms.md).
