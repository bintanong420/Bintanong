     > 1. Proceed to the Cashiering Office and inform teller of payment for Certificate of Good Moral Character (CGMC)

     > 2. Proceed to the Student Discipline Records Unit for endorsement
     > No fee · about 10 minutes · OSAS (OSAS Office Assistant)
     > What the office does: Provide endorsement; Instruct client to proceed to the Student Discipline Records Unit.
     > 3. Present the applicable required documents to the Student Discipline Unit (SDU) service provider
     > No fee · about 5 minutes · OSAS (College Guidance Counselor)
     > What the office does: Check record of requesting client.
     > 4. Give pertinent information: a. Degree or Course & Year Level b. Term & School Year (SY) Graduated or Last Attended c. Specific Purpose of the Certification
     > No fee · about 5 minutes · OSAS (College Guidance Counselor)
     > What the office does: Interview the client and get pertinent information; Prepare the document using the CGMC template.
     > 5. Proof read the entries in the Certificate of Good Moral Character
     > No fee · about 3 minutes · OSAS (College Guidance Counselor)
     > What the office does: Show the certification to the client for proof-reading.
     > 6. Receive the Certificate of Good Moral Character (CGMC)
     > No fee · about 1 minute · OSAS (College Guidance Counselor)
     > What the office does: Seal the certification; Issue the sealed certification.
     > 7. Sign the Transaction Sheets
     > No fee · about 1 minute · OSAS (College Guidance Counselor)
     > What the office does: Encode transaction in the CGMC Serial Number Tracking Form.
     > Total: Php 100.00 · about 30 minutes
